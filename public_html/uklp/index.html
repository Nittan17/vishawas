While primarily a privacy document, the inclusion of "Limitation of Liability" linked to fees (Section 6.2) serves a dual purpose. It acts as a de facto Terms of Service for the data interaction. By stating that government fees are non-refundable and liability is capped at the consultancy fee, the document pre-empts the most common financial dispute: clients demanding full refunds (including visa fees) after a rejection.
